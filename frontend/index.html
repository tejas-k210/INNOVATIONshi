The Challenge of EV Battery Second-Life

Why simple percentage health ratings fail to tell the true condition of aged lithium-ion packs.

The Conventional Blind Spot

Why a Single SOH % Is Insufficient

Commercial battery management systems typically output a single aggregate number like "82% SOH". However, two batteries with identical SOH ratings can have drastically different second-life viability:

  • Invisible Cell Weak Links: In a series pack, a single severely degraded cell (like Cell #14 with 51% SOH) dictates pack thermal runaway risk and early cut-off.
  • Unknown Degradation Physics: Capacity fade alone doesn't differentiate between reversible Loss of Lithium Inventory (LLI) and irreversible Loss of Active Material (LAM).
  • Arbitrary Retirement vs. Value Destruction: Without cycle projection and transparent reuse-vs-recycling valuation, high-value packs get prematurely shredded.
"A battery pack is only as safe and capable as its weakest series cell."
